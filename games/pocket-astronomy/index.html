How to play

1. Follow the compass. Scanned objects are ignored.
2. Zoom (+/-) or Pinch to focus on the target depth.
3. Equip the correct lens to decode the data coordinates.
4. Turn on the Sky guide (button in the Mission quiz card) and tap any star to study it.
5. In the Stellar Database, tap 📍 Locate on a scanned object to aim the telescope at its coordinates.

🎓 Mission quiz

Quiz yourself on what you have scanned in this sector and the Sky guide stars you have studied: +10 grants per correct answer. Turn on the Sky guide to draw real constellations, then tap any star, or centre one in the reticle and press Study star, to read about it.
How grants work: first discovery of each object, +25% survey bonus for completing a sector, follow-up observations (+10% of an object\u2019s value, once every 2 minutes per object, with the right sensor), quiz answers, and finishing constellations.
